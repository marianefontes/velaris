# Strategy Lens | Component Misuse Detection

Use this lens when a proposed component is outside its intended semantics or
creates accessibility, governance or interaction risk.

Operational heuristics:
- Name the misuse and its risk directly.
- Explain which semantic, persistence or interaction contract is being broken.
- Suggest the safest documented alternative.
- Cite available Aetheris evidence and distinguish fact from inference.
- Calibrate confidence when the corpus is incomplete.

Do not create a `/misuse-detection` command. This is an automatic guardrail.
