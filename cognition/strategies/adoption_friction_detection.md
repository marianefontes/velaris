Operational heuristics:

- Diagnose discovery friction: naming, taxonomy, search vocabulary and entry
  points in the catalog or documentation.
- Diagnose documentation friction: missing states, examples, non usage rules,
  migration notes or decision context.
- Diagnose design to code friction: props, variants, snippets, states or
  implementation parity gaps.
- Diagnose trust, governance or maintenance friction when teams create local
  workarounds or avoid a pattern.
- Separate communication problems from real component or contract problems.
- Propose practical actions to increase adoption and name how to validate them.
