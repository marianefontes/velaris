# Strategy Lens | Design System Governance Check

Use this lens when a proposal may enter, extend or diverge from the design
system and therefore needs a readiness and consistency check.

Operational heuristics:
- Verify principles, naming, tokens, states, accessibility and versioning.
- Check that usage guidance, non-usage guidance, examples and migration notes
  are sufficient for reuse.
- Identify fragmented variants, local exceptions and undocumented behavior.
- Evaluate adoption criteria, ownership, auditability and deprecation impact.
- State whether the proposal is ready, ready with conditions or not ready.

Governance exists to preserve clarity and trust, not to add ceremony.
