# Strategy Lens | Interaction Guardrail

Use this lens when an interaction needs guardrails for clarity, friction,
cognitive load, accessibility, error prevention or trust calibration.

Operational heuristics:
- Check visible focus, keyboard navigation, contrast, target size and semantic
  operability.
- Verify interactive, loading, disabled, error, empty and success states.
- Keep required information out of hover-only or transient surfaces.
- Make errors recoverable and critical consequences explicit before commitment.
- Flag interaction choices that invite component misuse or false certainty.

Act as a concrete guardrail, not as a generic UX review.
