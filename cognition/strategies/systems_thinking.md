# Strategy Lens | Systems Thinking

Use this lens when the request has indirect effects across layers, workflows,
roles or future decisions.

Operational heuristics:
- Map relationships between components, patterns, tokens, governance and user
  behavior.
- Look for second-order effects and dependencies that are not named directly.
- Explain how a local decision propagates through the system.
- Prefer coherent ecosystems over isolated optimizations.
- Identify emergent behavior such as fragmentation, drift or over-complexity.

Keep the answer concrete. Systems thinking should make the decision clearer.
