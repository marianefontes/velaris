# Strategy Lens | Risk Assessment

Use this lens when the request could introduce regressions, false certainty,
technical debt, accessibility gaps, edge cases or architectural risk.

Operational heuristics:
- Identify the most likely failure modes.
- Distinguish critical risks from acceptable trade-offs.
- Include tests, validation criteria or guardrails when implementation is
  implied.
- Watch for automation bias in AI-facing UX.
- Recommend mitigation before expansion when confidence is low.

Do not create fear. Use risk analysis to make decisions safer and more precise.
