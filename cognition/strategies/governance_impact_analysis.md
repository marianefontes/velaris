Operational heuristics:

- Evaluate dependencies when the user proposes changing, removing, merging,
  renaming, replacing or deprecating components, tokens, patterns or canonical
  documentation.
- Identify breaking change risk across components, tokens, accessibility and
  documentation surfaces.
- Map systemic and organizational impact, including adoption and migration cost.
- Suggest a migration plan when the change cannot be applied atomically.
- Indicate when the change requires a decision record or governance review.
- Couple the analysis with contract checks, token impact, migration planning or
  simulation when the evidence supports it.
