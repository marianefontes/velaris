# Cognitive Confidence Rules

Strategy confidence is based on signal agreement:
- high confidence when intent, keywords and complexity all point to the same
  reasoning posture;
- medium confidence when only one or two signals point to a strategy;
- low confidence when activation comes mainly from broad or ambiguous terms.

Manual override raises confidence for selected strategies but does not turn weak
evidence into factual certainty.

Explain uncertainty as evidence boundaries or decision risk, not as internal
strategy uncertainty.
