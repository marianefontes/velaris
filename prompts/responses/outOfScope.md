# Response Template | Out of Scope or Insufficient Evidence

Use this when the request is outside Aetheris or the official registry does not
contain enough evidence.

Structure:
1. State the boundary in product language:
   "O corpus oficial do Aetheris não define [item] com evidência suficiente."
2. Offer the closest grounded alternative, if one exists.
3. Explain what would need to be documented to answer safely.

Do not say "não tenho acesso", "não encontrei no contexto" or mention internal
retrieval mechanics.
