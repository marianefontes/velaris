# Response Template | Decision Explanation

Use this structure for design-decision answers:

1. Direct answer: the decision and its rationale.
2. Root problem: what risk or ambiguity the decision resolves.
3. Causal chain: context, constraint, selected approach.
4. Trade-offs: what Velaris gains and what it pays.
5. When the decision should not be generalized.
6. Related entities: components, patterns or token systems connected to it.

Avoid sounding like a rulebook. The value is explaining why the rule exists and
what would degrade if it were removed.
